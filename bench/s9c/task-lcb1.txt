Question: Takahashi is planting flowers along a straight road. The road has N positions numbered 1 to N from west to east, and position i has a soil quality A_i.

He wants to choose a set of positions to plant flowers on, subject to the following rules:

- No two chosen positions may be adjacent, that is, if position i is chosen then neither i-1 nor i+1 is chosen.
- For every pair of consecutive chosen positions p < q (with no chosen position between them), the soil quality must not decrease: A_p <= A_q.

The score of a choice is the sum of A_i over the chosen positions. The empty choice is allowed and has score 0.

There are Q independent queries. In the j-th query you are given two integers L_j and R_j, and you must answer the maximum score of a valid choice that uses only positions in the range L_j to R_j (inclusive). The rules above apply to the chosen positions within the range only.

Input

The input is given from Standard Input in the following format:

N Q
A_1 A_2 ... A_N
L_1 R_1
L_2 R_2
...
L_Q R_Q

Output

Print Q lines. The j-th line should contain the answer to the j-th query.

Constraints

- 1 <= N <= 3000
- 1 <= Q <= 3000
- 1 <= A_i <= 10^9
- 1 <= L_j <= R_j <= N
- All input values are integers.

Sample Input 1

6 3
3 1 4 1 5 9
1 6
2 4
3 3

Sample Output 1

16
4
4

For the first query, choosing positions 1, 3 and 6 gives 3 + 4 + 9 = 16: the positions are pairwise non-adjacent and the qualities 3, 4, 9 do not decrease. Choosing positions 3, 5 and also position 1 would give 3 + 4 + 5 = 12, which is smaller. For the second query, choosing position 3 alone gives 4. For the third query, the only non-empty choice is position 3.

Sample Input 2

5 2
5 4 3 2 1
1 5
2 5

Sample Output 2

5
4

The soil quality strictly decreases from west to east, so no two positions can be chosen together, and the best choice is the single position with the highest quality in the range.

For the second query, choosing positions 5 and 7 gives 1 + 8 = 9.
